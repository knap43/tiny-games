# HOLLYHOCK

A tiny cozy farming sim in four seasons. Your aunt left you Hollyhock Farm, overgrown and quiet. The township lease falls due at the end of every season (500G, 1500G, 3000G, 4500G); pay all four and the farm is yours.

```
python hollyhock.py            # fullscreen
python hollyhock.py --windowed
```

**Controls:**
- **WASD / arrows:** walk. **Hold Space:** use the selected tool or item on the tile you face. **E:** talk, harvest, pet, collect, ship, sleep.
- **1-9, 0, -, =** (or Q/R, or the mouse wheel) pick a bag slot; **Tab** swaps between the two bag rows.
- Menus: W/S and E, Shift+E buys five. Esc pauses or leaves, F11 toggles fullscreen.

**Farming:** clear the debris with the axe, pickaxe and scythe (for wood, stone and hay), till with the hoe, plant, and water every day: crops only grow on days they were watered. Twelve crops across the seasons, from turnips and strawberries to melons, pumpkins, cranberries and winter kale. Anything out of season withers when the season turns. Harvest with E or the scythe, and drop goods in the shipping bin by the house; it pays out overnight. Each day runs in real time from 6:00; you can stay up past midnight, but you'll wake tired, and at 2:00 you'll fall asleep where you stand.

**Weather:** rain waters your fields, storms can bring lightning down on a crop, and in winter it snows and the pond freezes, so you'll want a well.

**Threats:** crows strip unguarded fields at dawn, unless a scarecrow stands within four tiles. By day, rabbits hop in to nibble your crops; walk over and they scatter.

**The village** lies east along the road:
- **Marigold** sells seeds and tea (8:00-18:00).
- **Bram** builds coops, barns, wells and a greenhouse, makes scarecrows and sprinklers, upgrades your hoe and watering can, and sells wood and stone (9:00-17:00).
- **Hazel** sells chickens, cows, sheep and hay (7:00-16:00).

Talk to them each day and bring gifts (each has two favourites) to earn hearts: five hearts gets you a friend's discount, and eight brings a present.

**Animals** each eat one hay every morning and give eggs, milk or wool. Pet them daily and they'll give large eggs, large milk and fine wool. They head inside at dusk.

The game saves every night. Your best total harvest is remembered.
