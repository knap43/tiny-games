# VIGIL

A tiny dark-fantasy wave-survival roguelike in the spirit of Brotato and Vampire Survivors, drawn in GRAVEN's black, bone and blood. A lone knight keeps vigil on an endless graveyard moor while the dead come in waves.

```
python vigil.py            # fullscreen
python vigil.py --windowed
```

**Controls:** WASD/arrows move · your weapons strike on their own · Esc pause · F11 fullscreen/window. In menus: A/D (and W/S in the shop) to choose, E or Enter to confirm, or use the mouse.

**A run:** pick a starting weapon, then survive timed waves (20 seconds at first, up to 60). A blinking red ✕ marks where something is about to claw its way up. Kills drop souls, which are both experience and currency. When a wave ends, the souls on the field fly to you and you choose a boon for every level gained, then visit the Pedlar. Health is restored at the start of every wave. Every fifth wave **The Pale Shepherd** comes: rings and spirals of bolts, summoned ghouls and a telegraphed charge, and it will not be outrun. The wave ends when it falls. The night never ends; your longest vigil is remembered.

**Weapons** (up to six, circling the knight; tiers I–IV): dagger (fast, crits often), sword (sweeping arc), spear (long piercing thrust), scythe (wide reaping sweep), crossbow (piercing bolts), wand (bursting firebolts), hexstaff (a hex leaping between foes), throwing axe (cuts going out and coming back), skulls (orbit you).

**The Pedlar** sells weapons and relics. Buying a weapon when your hands are full combines it with a matching one; select an owned weapon to sell it (E or click) or combine two of the same weapon and tier into the next tier (C or right click). R rerolls the offers.

**Foes:** ghouls, bat swarms, cultists who keep their distance and cast, hounds that crouch and lunge, armoured revenants, and bloaters that burst when killed (step out of the red ring). Damage numbers are white, red for critical hits.
